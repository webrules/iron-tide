BUILD ORDER

Deploy your MCV. Build a power plant, then a refinery (includes a miner). Add a barracks, war factory, and shipyard. Place buildings near your existing base; shipyards go in coastal water.

WINNING THE WAR

Cruisers bombard land and sea. Submarines detect nearby enemy subs; Allied destroyers detect from farther away. Detected subs can be hit by cruisers. Coastal guns need power.

Move a transport near your units and right-click it to board (6 cargo slots). Sail to a beach and unload. Tanks and miners use 2 slots; MCVs use 3. Deploy another MCV on the central island to expand.

Ore regenerates. Low power slows production. Repairs cost credits; selling returns half a structure’s price. No fog of war, aircraft, capture, superweapons, or saving.
